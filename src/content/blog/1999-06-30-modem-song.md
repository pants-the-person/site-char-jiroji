The modem is singing again.

It starts with a dial tone and then a series of beeps, like a robot trying to remember a phone number. Then the static. Then the handshake, which sounds like two machines agreeing to be friends.

I recorded it with the microphone that came with the computer. The quality is bad. That is the point.

You can hear:

- the dial
- the carrier tone
- my fan in the background
- a cat (not me)

I listen to it when the internet is slow. It reminds me that connection is a process and you have to be patient.

If you have a recording of your modem, e-mail me. I am collecting them. I do not know why. It seems like the kind of thing I should do.
